# The Great Emu War (1932) — notes

Sources:
- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War
- Britannica: https://www.britannica.com/topic/Emu-War
- National Geographic: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/australian-emu-war-history
- Australian Geographic: https://australiangeographic.com.au/?p=23036

Facts used (sources agreeing):
- November 1932, Campion district, Western Australia (all four).
- ~20,000 emus on the wheat farms (all four: "approximately/roughly/at least/more than 20,000").
- Three soldiers: Major G.P.W. Meredith, Sgt S. McMurray, Gunner J. O'Halloran (Wiki, Brit, NG).
- Two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds (Wiki, Brit, AG; NG two Lewis guns).
- Ambush at a dam/watering hole, gun jammed (all four).
- Gun mounted on a truck; truck couldn't keep up on rough terrain, gunner couldn't fire (Wiki, Brit, AG).
- Emus split into small groups with a lookout (Wiki, Brit, NG).
- Meredith: emus "could face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks" (all four).
- Recalled within a week (8 Nov) (Wiki, Brit, AG); returned 12–13 Nov.
- 986 kills claimed, with 9,860 rounds = 10 per bird (Wiki; Brit gives 986; both say disputed). Script says "claimed".
- Minimal impact on the emu population (Wiki); emus called the victors (Wiki, Brit, NG, AG).
- Bounties: ~57,000 claimed in six months of 1934 (Wiki 57,034; Brit "roughly 57,000").
- Not used: top speed (NG says 55 mph, others give none).
