In nineteen thirty two, Australia declared war on birds. And lost. Twenty thousand emus were eating the wheat farms of Western Australia. So the army sent three soldiers, two machine guns and ten thousand bullets. First, they set an ambush at a watering hole. A huge flock walked right in. Then the gun jammed. Next, they bolted a machine gun onto a truck. The emus just ran. On the rough ground the truck couldn't keep up, and the gunner couldn't aim. The birds split into small groups, each with a lookout. The major in charge said they faced machine guns like tanks. After one week, the army retreated. They came back and claimed nine hundred and eighty six kills. That's ten bullets for every bird. It barely made a dent. So farmers went back to bounties, and claimed fifty seven thousand in six months. The emus had won the war. Subscribe for more weird history.
