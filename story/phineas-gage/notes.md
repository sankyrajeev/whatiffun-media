Facts (≥ 2 sources each)
- 13 Sept 1848, near Cavendish, Vermont; railroad construction foreman (Britannica, Akron, Wikipedia).
- Age 25 (NPR "25-year-old"; born July 1823 per Britannica/Wikipedia).
- Tamping iron 3 ft 7 in (~1.09 m, "about a metre"), 1.25 in diameter, 13.25 lb ≈ 6 kg (Britannica, Wikipedia; Akron 13.5 lb).
- Entered below the left cheekbone, behind the eye, out the top of the skull (Britannica, NPR).
- Landed ~25 m (80 ft) away (Britannica "almost 82 feet (25 metres)", Wikipedia "80 feet (25 meters)"; Akron 25–30 yards).
- Conscious and spoke within minutes (Britannica, Wikipedia).
- Friends said he was "no longer Gage"; rude, profane (Harlow quoted by NPR, Britannica, Akron, Wikipedia).
- Case helped link frontal lobes to personality (NPR, Britannica).
- Stagecoach driver in Chile ~1852–1859, "nearly seven" years (Britannica, Akron, NPR).
- Died May 1860 of epileptic seizures, ~12 years after (Britannica, NPR).
- Skull and iron on display at Harvard Medical School's Warren Anatomical Museum (Britannica, NPR).
