# meteorite-nap — Ann Hodges, the woman hit by a meteorite (1954)

Category: weird history / fun facts. STORY B 2026-11-26 (A = db-cooper, mystery).

## Facts (≥ 2 sources each)
- 30 Nov 1954, Sylacauga (Oak Grove), Alabama — Wikipedia, Smithsonian, Encyclopedia of Alabama, NatGeo.
- Napping on her couch/sofa under quilts — Smithsonian, NatGeo, EoA, Wikipedia.
- Fireball seen in three states (Alabama, Georgia, Mississippi) — Wikipedia ("three states"), Smithsonian (names them).
- Rock ~8.5 lb = 3.86 kg ("four kilos") — Wikipedia, Smithsonian, EoA.
- Through the roof/ceiling, bounced off a (console) radio, hit her (hip/thigh/side), large bruise — all four.
- ~200 reporters/sightseers by nightfall — Wikipedia, EoA.
- Police chief took it, handed to the Air Force (Maxwell) — Wikipedia, NatGeo, EoA (Smithsonian: Air Force took custody).
- Landlady Birdie Guy sued; settled for $500 paid to Guy so Hodges kept it — Smithsonian, NatGeo, Wikipedia.
- Donated to the Alabama Museum of Natural History by 1956 — EoA, NatGeo, Wikipedia.
- Farmer Julius McKinney found a second fragment in a (dirt) road the next day (1 Dec) — Wikipedia, EoA; sold it,
  bought a car and a house — Wikipedia, Smithsonian (EoA: small farm and used car).
- Only person known to be injured by a meteorite — Smithsonian, NatGeo ("only confirmed person hit").
