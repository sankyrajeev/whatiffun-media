# What if all the gold ever mined was melted into one cube? (STORY A, 2026-11-27, What if)

## Facts and sources
- ~222,600 t of gold mined throughout history, ~two-thirds since 1950; above-ground stock 224,200 t (end-Q3 2026).
  Cube "around 22.6 metres on each side". — World Gold Council, https://www.gold.org/goldhub/data/how-much-gold
  Cross-check: own maths below (density 19.32 g/cm³); older totals at https://www.visualcapitalist.com/chart-how-much-gold-is-in-the-world/ and https://www.bullionstar.com/blogs/gold-silver-101/how-much-gold-is-there-in-the-world/ give the same ~22 m cube.
- Gold price ~$3,579/oz on 26 Nov 2026 — https://www.usagold.com/daily-precious-metals-market-report-november-26-2026/ ; https://www.wam.ae/en/article/17joik3-gold-prices-steady-near-3567-per-ounce
- Eiffel Tower total weight 10,100 t — https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/key-figures ; https://www.britannica.com/topic/Eiffel-Tower-Paris-France
- World population ~8.3 billion (2026) — https://www.census.gov/data/data-tools/population-clock/world-notes.html ; https://statisticstimes.com/demographics/world-population.php
- Fritz Haber, 1920s: tried to extract gold from seawater to pay Germany's WWI reparations; first estimate (65 mg per tonne)
  was ~1,000× too high; abandoned because extraction cost more than the gold. —
  https://gizmodo.com/germany-s-post-world-war-i-scheme-to-extract-gold-from-499793752 ;
  https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/gold-ocean-sea-hoax-science-water-boom-rush-treasure

  gives ~1 g per 100 million t of water, which implies far less; the figures conflict, so not used.

## Maths (python)
- volume = 222,600 t / 19.32 t/m³ = 11,522 m³ → side 22.59 m ("twenty-two and a half metres")
- 22.6 m / 3.2 m per storey ≈ 7 storeys
- 222,600 / 10,100 = 22.0 Eiffel Towers
- 222,600 t × 32,150.75 oz/t = 7.157 bn oz × $3,579 = $25.6 trillion ("about twenty-five trillion")
- per person: 222,600e6 g / 8.25e9 = 27 g; $25.6tn / 8.25e9 ≈ $3,100 ("about three thousand dollars")
