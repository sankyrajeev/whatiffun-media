# Thai cave rescue (Tham Luang, 2018) — notes

Sources:
- History.com, "Thai soccer team rescued from cave": https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/july-10/thai-soccer-team-rescued-from-cave
- National Geographic, "The untold story of the daring cave divers who saved the Thai soccer team": https://www.nationalgeographic.com/adventure/article/thai-rescue-cave-divers
- Wikipedia, "Tham Luang cave rescue": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tham_Luang_cave_rescue
- CNN, "Thai cave rescue: the full story": https://edition.cnn.com/2018/07/11/asia/thai-cave-rescue-full-story-intl

Facts used (≥ 2 sources each):
- 12 boys (11–16) + 25-year-old assistant coach entered on 23 June 2018 after practice (History, Wikipedia; CNN date).
- Early monsoon rain flooded the cave behind them (History, Wikipedia "a month earlier than usual", NatGeo).
- Found 2 July by two British divers (Volanthen, Stanton) — 9 days by the dates (History "nine days"; Wikipedia/CNN date).
- ~4 km inside (Wikipedia, CNN; History 2.5 miles).
- Saman Kunan, former Thai Navy SEAL, died 6 July after running out of air delivering tanks (History, CNN, Wikipedia "asphyxiation"; NatGeo says cause unclear, so the script says only "runs out of air ... and dies", which 3 sources state).
- Boys sedated (ketamine, Dr Richard Harris), wetsuit + full-face mask, carried out by divers (History, NatGeo, Wikipedia).
- Out 4 on 8 July, 4 on 9 July, last 4 + coach on 10 July; all 13 survived (History, Wikipedia).
- "Thousands of volunteers" (Wikipedia ~10,000 people; NatGeo "more than 7,000 volunteers") — exact number left out because sources differ.
- Left out: 17 vs 18 total days (History vs Wikipedia disagree); 9 vs 10 days to find (said "nine", matches dates).
