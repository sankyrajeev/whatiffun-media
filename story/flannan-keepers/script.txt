Three men vanished from a lighthouse, and nobody knows why.
December, nineteen hundred. A tiny rock off the west coast of Scotland. A passing ship notices the Flannan Isles light has gone dark.
Storms keep the relief boat away. It finally lands eleven days later, on Boxing Day. Nobody waves. No flag flies.
Joseph Moore climbs up alone. The gate is shut. The door is shut. The beds are unmade, and the clock has stopped.
Then a clue. One set of oilskins is still inside. So one man ran out in just his shirt sleeves.
Down at the west landing, a box of supplies is smashed and the turf is torn up.
The inspector's verdict? A huge wave swept all three men off the rock. Their bodies were never found.
And those spooky logbook entries about storms and praying? There's no evidence they ever existed. Someone added them years later.
