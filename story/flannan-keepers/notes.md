# Flannan Isles lighthouse keepers, December 1900 (Mystery)

Facts used (each from ≥ 2 sources):
- Three keepers: James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, (Donald/William) MacArthur — RMG, History Hit, History UK.
- 15 Dec 1900: SS Archtor passes and sees no light — NRS (Captain Holman's report), History Hit, History UK.
- Relief vessel Hesperus delayed by weather, lands 26 Dec (Boxing Day), "11 days later" — NRS, History Hit, History UK.
- No flag, no welcome — RMG, History Hit.
- Joseph Moore went ashore; gate and door closed, beds unmade, clock stopped — RMG, History Hit.
- One set of oilskins left behind → one man went out in shirt sleeves — RMG, History Hit, History UK.
- West landing damage: supply box destroyed, turf ripped up — RMG, History Hit.
- Superintendent Robert Muirhead: an extra-large sea swept them away at the west landing — History Hit, History UK (quotes his report).
- No bodies found — History Hit ("No bodies were ever found"), History UK ("no sign of the three keepers").
- Dramatic log entries (storm, weeping, praying, "God is over all") not genuine: History UK (no evidence they ever existed, added years later; Mike Dash, Fortean Times), History Hit ("no such entries had ever been made"), RMG (no storms reported, "made up, or localised").

Left out (single source or disputed): meal on the table, toppled chair, canary, 160 steps, the 110 ft box height.
