# Mike the Headless Chicken (Story B, 2026-11-28, Weird history)

Sources:
- https://www.britannica.com/story/how-mike-the-chicken-survived-without-a-head
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_the_Headless_Chicken
- https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/mike-chicken-lived-18-months-without-head-180952891/
- https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2024/2/the-unbelievable-tale-of-the-chicken-that-lived-headless-for-over-a-year-764825
- https://www.mentalfloss.com/animals/birds/mike-the-headless-chicken-sideshow-oddity

Facts used (≥ 2 sources each):
- 10 Sept 1945, Lloyd Olsen, Fruita, Colorado (Britannica, Wikipedia, Guinness, Mental Floss).
- Mother-in-law/visiting mother wanted the neck, so the blow was high (Wikipedia, Mental Floss, Smithsonian).
- Brain stem left intact (Wikipedia, Mental Floss, Smithsonian; Britannica: the part controlling breathing and digestion).
- Fed milk + water by eyedropper (Wikipedia, Smithsonian, Mental Floss); throat cleared with a syringe (Britannica, Guinness).
- Toured the US, 25 cents admission (Wikipedia, Guinness, Mental Floss, Smithsonian "a quarter").
- Time and Life coverage (Wikipedia, Guinness; Britannica/Smithsonian Time, Mental Floss Life).
- Lived 18 months (Britannica, Wikipedia, Smithsonian, Guinness).
- Died March 1947 choking in a Phoenix motel; syringe left at the sideshow (Britannica, Wikipedia, Guinness; Smithsonian/Mental Floss: eyedropper/syringe left behind).
- Annual festival in Fruita (Britannica, Wikipedia, Guinness, Mental Floss); Guinness record longest surviving headless chicken (Guinness, Wikipedia).

Left out (single source or conflicting): $4,500/month and $10,000 value (Wikipedia only), weight 2.5 → 8 lb (Mental Floss only), the ear, festival start year, Hope Wade.
