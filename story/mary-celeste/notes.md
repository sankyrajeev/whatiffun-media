# Mary Celeste (1872) — notes

Sources:
- Smithsonian: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/abandoned-ship-the-mary-celeste-174488104/
- Royal Museums Greenwich: https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/maritime-history/mystery-mary-celeste
- History.com: https://www.history.com/articles/what-happened-to-the-mary-celeste
- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Celeste

Facts used (sources agreeing):
- Left New York 7 Nov 1872 for Genoa (S, R, H, W).
- 10 aboard: Capt. Benjamin Briggs, wife Sarah, daughter Sophia (2), seven crew (S, R, W; H says 8 crew — outlier).
- Found drifting near the Azores by the Dei Gratia, 4/5 Dec 1872 (S, R, H, W). Script says "December", "near the Azores".
- Only lifeboat missing (all four).
- Six months of food and water aboard (S, H); "amply provisioned" (W).
- Belongings left (S, R, W).
- ~1,700 barrels of alcohol: 1,701 (S, W), ~1,700 (R, H). Script: "seventeen hundred".
- Last log entry 25 Nov off Santa Maria, Azores (S, R, H, W); found nine days later (H, W), ~400–500 miles away → "hundreds of miles".
- Pump disassembled (S, R); sounding rod on deck (R, W); 3.5 ft of water in hold (S, W).
- Theory: false sounding / thought she was sinking (W Deveau; S pump; H clogged pump). Framed as "maybe".
- Nobody ever seen again (all four).
- Sails half set: W/S describe partial sail ("some sails set", torn). Kept as "half set".
