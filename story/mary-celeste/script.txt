In eighteen seventy-two, a ship was found sailing with nobody on board. December. Sailors spot the Mary Celeste drifting near the Azores, her sails half set. Ten people had left New York on her: the captain, his wife, their two-year-old daughter, and seven crew. All gone. The only lifeboat was missing. But six months of food and water were still on board. So were their clothes. And seventeen hundred barrels of alcohol. The last log entry was November twenty-fifth, off an island in the Azores. Nine days later, the ship turned up hundreds of miles away. So why leave a ship that wasn't sinking? One clue. A pump lay in pieces, and a sounding rod was left on deck. There was water in the hold. Three and a half feet.
