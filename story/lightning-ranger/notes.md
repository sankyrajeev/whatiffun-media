# Roy Sullivan — the man struck by lightning seven times (STORY B, 2026-11-23, Abnormal humans)

## Sources
- Guinness World Records, "Incredible story of man who survived being struck by lightning seven times" (2023): https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/1/incredible-story-of-man-who-survived-being-struck-by-lightning-seven-times-733932
- Wikipedia, Roy Sullivan: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Sullivan
- Mental Floss, "Meet the man struck by lightning 7 times": https://www.mentalfloss.com/science/weather/meet-man-struck-lightning-7-times
- US National Weather Service, lightning odds: https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-odds

## Facts used (each in >= 2 sources)
- Shenandoah National Park ranger in Virginia; struck 7 times 1942–1977 (Guinness, Wikipedia, Mental Floss).
- 1942: fire lookout tower; right leg burned, big toe hit/toenail lost (all three).
- 1969: in his truck; eyebrows burned off, knocked unconscious (all three).
- 1970: front yard / garden; left shoulder seared (all three).
- Hair set on fire in 1972, 1973, 1976 (Guinness + Wikipedia; Wikipedia adds 1977) -> "three separate times".
- 1973: a shoe knocked off (Wikipedia + Mental Floss).
- 1977: fishing; chest and stomach burned (all three); then a bear, hit with a branch (Wikipedia + Mental Floss).
- Guinness record holder for most lightning strikes survived (Guinness + Wikipedia).
- Odds of being struck in a lifetime (80 years): NWS 1 in 15,300; Guinness "around 1 in 10,000" -> script says "about one in fifteen thousand" (NWS figure).

## Maths
- 1942 -> 1969 = 27 years ("twenty-seven years later").
- 7 strikes over 35 years (1942–1977).

## Left out on purpose
- How he died (1983) — not part of the story, not appropriate for this format.
- Disputed details (toe "knocked off" vs toenail; wristwatch; 1977 pool vs boat).
