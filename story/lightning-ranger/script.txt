One man was struck by lightning seven times. He lived through every one. Roy Sullivan was a park ranger in the Virginia mountains. Your odds of being hit even once in a lifetime are about one in fifteen thousand. In nineteen forty-two, a bolt hit his fire lookout tower. It burned his leg and his big toe. Twenty-seven years later it found him in his truck and burned off his eyebrows. The next summer it hit him in his garden and seared his shoulder. Then it set his hair on fire. Three separate times. One strike knocked the shoe right off his foot. In nineteen seventy-seven he went fishing. Strike number seven burned his chest and stomach. Then a bear came for his fish, and he hit it with a branch. Guinness still lists him as the person struck by lightning more times than anyone else on record. Subscribe for more weird humans.
