# Chile 33 — the San José mine rescue (Rescue, STORY A 2026-11-22)

Sources
- History.com: https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/october-13/chilean-miners-are-rescued-after-69-days-underground
- Wikipedia/Wikiwand: https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/2010_Copiapo_mining_accident
- Maclean's: https://macleans.ca/news/world/miracle-men/
- Britannica (listing): https://www.britannica.com/event/Chile-mine-rescue-of-2010

Facts used (sources)
- Collapse 5 Aug 2010, San José gold & copper mine, Atacama/Copiapó — History, Wikiwand.
- ~700 m deep — Wikiwand "700 meters (2,300 ft)"; History "nearly half a mile"; Maclean's "more than 600 m".
- Emergency shelter, food for a few days stretched by rationing — History "several days' worth", Wikiwand "two or three days" lasting ~two weeks; Maclean's two spoonfuls of tuna every two days.
- 17 days without contact, drill broke through 22 Aug, note "We are fine in the refuge, the 33" — History, Wikiwand.
- Supplies down a narrow borehole in tubes ("palomas") — History (bore hole), Wikiwand (palomas).
- Capsule painted in Chile's flag colours — History; Fénix 2 capsule — Wikiwand.
- ~15 min per ride — History "around 15 minutes"; Wikiwand 9–18 minutes.
- 69 days; last man Luis Urzúa on 13 Oct; all 33 survived — Wikiwand, Maclean's, History.
