# The doctor who drank bacteria (Barry Marshall, H. pylori) — STORY B 2026-11-22

Category: Weird history / fun facts nobody knows.

## Facts (≥ 2 sources each)
- Ulcers were blamed on stress and excess acid — Nobel press release, SciAm, Discover, Lasker, Wikipedia.
- Warren (pathologist) and Marshall in Australia (Perth) linked a spiral bacterium to gastritis/ulcers — Nobel, Wikipedia.
- July 1984, Marshall aged 32 (born 30 Sep 1951) drank a culture — Lasker ("July 1984, a 32-year-old doctor"), Wikipedia (1984, birth date), SciAm ("summer of 1984").
- He did not tell his wife beforehand — Lasker, SciAm.
- Bad breath within ~3 days, then morning vomiting — SciAm, Wikipedia, Discover, Lasker.
- Endoscopy ~10 days in showed gastritis and bacteria everywhere — Discover, Lasker (Wikipedia: day 8; script says "ten days in").
- Cleared with antibiotics — Discover, Lasker, SciAm, Wikipedia.
- Ulcers now cured with a short antibiotic course instead of chronic acid-suppressing treatment — Nobel press release, Wikipedia.
- 2005 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, Marshall and J. Robin Warren — Nobel press release, Wikipedia.
- Avoided: the Easter 1982 culture story (sources disagree on which sample/how many days).
